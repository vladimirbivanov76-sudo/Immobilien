01 / THE ISLAND

The gem
of the Ionian.

A beautiful climate, striking landscapes and the warmth of Greek life make Lefkada a special place to call your own.

Enjoy delicious local food, explore the coastline and discover an island connected to mainland Greece by a floating bridge. Preveza Airport offers another convenient route for visitors.

01

Mediterranean living

A climate suited to enjoying life and delicious food throughout the year.

02

Natural beauty

Memorable landscapes and a beautiful Ionian spirit.

03

Easy access

A floating bridge to the mainland, and the airport just 53 km from the properties.
